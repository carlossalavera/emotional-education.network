Institutional Membership 2026

Architecting the future of Emotional Education

Join a prestigious network of professionals and institutions dedicated to the advancement of Emotional Education through empirical research, pedagogical innovation, and ethical leadership.

Why become a member?

  • • European visibility for your institution or research group.
  • • Access to shared resources, protocols and training materials.
  • • Priority invitation to webinars, meetings and annual activities.
  • • Opportunities to join Erasmus+, Horizon Europe and research consortia.
  • • Possibility of co-authoring publications, reports and special issues.
  • • Networking with universities, schools, teacher-training centres and institutions.
  • • Participation in working groups on Emotional Education.

Who can join?

  • • Universities
  • • Research groups
  • • Schools and teacher-training centres
  • • Public institutions
  • • NGOs and educational organisations
  • • Individual researchers and educators

Membership is free

Joining the network does not involve any membership fee or financial commitment. The purpose is to promote collaboration, shared knowledge and joint initiatives.
