Research

We conduct continuous and advanced research in Emotional Education, generating high-impact scientific knowledge to inform evidence-based practices across European educational systems.

European Projects

The European Emotional Education Network actively promotes, develops, and coordinates European-funded initiatives, with a particular focus on Horizon Europe and Erasmus+ Key Action 2 (KA220) Cooperation Partnerships, fostering collaborative research, innovation, and knowledge transfer across institutions.
