Privacy Policy

The European Emotional Education Network is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all individuals and institutions interacting with the network.

1. Data Controller

European Emotional Education Network
Contact: info@emotionaleducationnetwork.com

2. Data We Collect

  • Full name
  • Institution or organization
  • Email address
  • Country
  • Information voluntarily provided through forms or communications

3. Purpose of Data Processing

The data will be used exclusively for:

  • Managing membership applications
  • Facilitating communication among members and institutions
  • Informing users about activities, conferences, projects, and publications
  • Managing academic and scientific collaborations
  • Sending newsletters and communications related to Emotional Education

4. Data Retention

Data will only be stored for the period necessary to maintain the academic or institutional relationship with the network.

5. Data Protection

The European Emotional Education Network adopts appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure the security and confidentiality of information.

6. User Rights

Users may request at any time:

  • Access to their personal data
  • Rectification
  • Deletion
  • Restriction of processing
  • Withdrawal of consent

Requests should be sent to:
info@emotionaleducationnetwork.com

7. Data Sharing

The European Emotional Education Network will not sell or transfer personal data to third parties for commercial purposes.
